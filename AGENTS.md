# development

- static browser game with vendored three.js r160 in `vendor/three/`; no runtime build step.
- run `python3 devserver.py` from this folder for the no-cache server on port 5179.
- check changed javascript with `node --check <file>` and run `git diff --check`.
- verify in a browser. local-only `window.__rawah` hooks (`begin`, `goto`, `teleport`, `setHour`, `look`, `press`, `hold`, `step`, `map`, `travel(name)`, `weather(regime)`) drive deterministic checks when pointer lock is unavailable; `step(n)` only renders the last frame. keep the hooks disabled on public hosts.
- world data in `data/` is generated by `tools/bake_world.py` from real sources (USGS lidar, Sentinel-2, Overture/OpenStreetMap); edit the script and re-bake rather than hand-editing `world.json` or the binaries. downloads and expensive rasters cache in `tools/.cache/`.
- `World.height()` is lidar (`baseHeight`, quad split along the (c, r)-(c+1, r+1) diagonal) plus micro-relief from `src/detail.js`; the CDLOD vertex shader computes the same thing. keep the JS and GLSL halves of `detail.js` in lock-step (same hashing, octaves, offsets, weights) or the hiker will float or sink.
- `World.cover()` turns the baked Sentinel-2 land cover (live conifer, burned, aspen, rock) plus terrain into the weights flora uses; keep tree placement driven by it so trees match the satellite colour underneath.
- names come only from OpenStreetMap data; do not invent names for unnamed lakes or features.
- all sound is synthesized in `src/audio.js`; no audio files.
- trees are generated at startup in `src/trees.js` (painted atlas, card meshes, impostor bake); flora placement stays in `src/flora.js`. species: lodgepole below a line that moves with aspect and drainage (about 2900-3100 m), then spruce and subalpine fir (fir share rises with elevation), aspen from the satellite, krummholz at treeline. saplings (scale under `SAPLING`) are drawn only close up and are never impostors. impostor atlas has 32 cells; keep the variant count under that.
- rock is `src/rocks.js` (three shapes, hex colours are sRGB, vertex colours linear) placed by `Flora.#populate`: scatter, outcrops, tors above treeline, talus fans under steep rock, erratics. formations are built from the neighbouring cells too, and each tile keeps only the rocks inside it, so they stay seamless; keep that if you change them. big blocks go in `solids` so you can't walk through them. the near-ground pebble shader (`blocky` in `src/terrain.js`) is separate.
- camera feel lives in `src/camera.js` and is driven by `Hiker.phase` (PI per footstep); keep footstep audio and head bob on that same phase. `V` toggles first/third person; `src/avatar.js` is a placeholder meant to be replaced by a rigged model.
- the sky draws clouds as a five-slice slab (`slab()` in `src/sky.js`): lower slices wide and dark, upper ones shrinking to billowing tops, each looking toward the sun for self-shadow, plus a thin cirrus layer; slice 0's footprint matches `cloudDensity`, so the ground shadows still line up. keep `CLOUD_GLSL` and its JS twin `cloudDensityAt` in step if the density function changes.
- weather is a continuous function of time, `sampleWeather` in `src/weather.js` (slow systems, afternoon heating and instability, drifting cloud banks, dawn fog), seeded per game and tilted by the season's climate in `calendar.js`; `daySchedule`/`forecast` for the journal are read off the same function, and the named `REGIMES` are only what the settings menu can hold. the colour grade (vibrance, S-curve, split tone) is folded into the ACES tone mapper at the top of `main.js` via `THREE.ShaderChunk.tonemapping_pars_fragment`; change it there. weather state is a few numbers (cloud, thick, precip, fog, wind, temp, storm) eased toward the day's seeded schedule; everything else reads them. cloud density has one GLSL source (`CLOUD_GLSL` in `src/atmosphere.js`) used by the sky and the ground's cloud shadows, plus a two-octave JS twin (`cloudDensityAt`); keep them in step.
- materials join the shared light and weather through `withAtmosphere(shader, { translucency, weather })`; don't fork per-material fog, cloud shadows, wetness or snow.
- gamepad input uses the standard mapping (what an Xbox controller reports). game actions go through the same key codes as the keyboard (`PAD` in `src/input.js`); menus and the map take named buttons via `Input.onPad`.
- the date lives in `src/calendar.js`; seasonal looks (leaves, grass curing, snowpack, lake ice, meadow green-up) come from it, not from hard-coded months elsewhere. the season is a setting and changing it reloads, because the tree atlas and impostors are painted for the season at boot.
- UI type: Gabarito for interface text, Paytone One for names and titles (vendored in `fonts/`; canvases use the same two, and `main.js` waits for them before painting). the logo is `assets/brand/rawah.png` (white wordmark) and the favicon `assets/brand/icon.png`. don't add other fonts.
- the map and field guide are one window (`src/map.js`, `#map` in index.html): the map is the satellite picture tinted navy with peach trails (`build`), places are markers you click or tap to get a card, and the list only appears for search or a filter. the field guide tab (`#guide`) shows lakes and summits from `state.discovered`, the wildlife, flora, checkpoints and easter eggs from `src/guide.js`. `guide.js` checks what the player stands in twice a second (`feedGuide` in main.js) and rolls sightings with a per-entry `chance`; keep the entry facts (`cover`, `dusk`, `alpine`...) in step with what `feedGuide` builds. mouse, touch, keyboard and pad all go through the same selection.
- the player body is `src/character.js`: a read-only view of `Hiker` (mode, stance, action, speed) that picks clips from `src/anims.js` and crossfades them. clips are Mixamo FBX in `assets/characters/anims/`, loaded lazily (`CORE` before the model shows, `EXTRA` after), with hip x/z travel stripped at load so the controller owns movement. characters face +z. the GLB export has no metalness, so `character.js` forces it to 0 (glTF defaults to fully metal, which renders black). `?char=grant` picks the other model. add moves by adding the clip to `anims.js`, a state to `Hiker`, and a branch in `Character.#want`.
- the other two friends are `src/companions.js` (`Party`): each is a `Character` posed from a `Body` that mimics the Hiker fields it reads (`face` and `emote` let a script turn them or hold a clip). they follow you, and every 30-70 s the pair walks a few metres off and chats, sits, or play-fights (punches plus hit reactions). they share the player's `AnimLibrary`. whoever you are not playing as is a companion (`?char=` picks the player).
- character GLBs: `tools/characters/convert.html` + `convert.mjs` load a Mixamo-rigged FBX with three's FBXLoader (serve a folder holding `<name>.fbx`, `<name>_dl.jpg` and a three checkout; run `node convert.mjs blaize grant corey` with playwright-core), merge vertices, stand the Z-up rig up (root rotation -90 degrees about X, scale 0.01) and export with GLTFExporter. The rigs come out Z-up with their own bone frames while the animation clips are Y-up Mixamo, so `Character` re-fits every clip to its rig (`retarget` in `src/anims.js`: each bone's turn from rest in the world, carried onto the rig's rest; hips height scaled). Without that the body twists or lies down. `RAWAH_RETARGET=false` turns it off for comparison. the top joint Mixamo shows over the head is a leaf end bone and does nothing.
- characters are lit by `skinLight` in `character.js`: shared fog, cloud shadow and sky occlusion via `withAtmosphere`, a sky-tinted rim, and baked texture highlights eased down. keep new character materials going through it.
- fighting lives in `Hiker` (`ACTIONS`, `#combat`, `receiveHit`): punches emit `strike` events with a direction and reach for whatever ends up doing hit detection; nothing in the world takes hits yet.
- the three loaders in `vendor/three/examples/jsm/` (GLTF, FBX with NURBS and fflate) are copied unmodified from three r160.
- grass remembers the hiker in a 40-slot ring of pressed capsules (`#trample` in `src/grass.js`, `uTrA`/`uTrB` in the vertex shader). `grass.update` takes the body heading from `avatar.heading`. lying and sitting renew their patch every frame so it stays flat under you; everything else springs back (`HOLD`, `SPRING`). keep the slot count in the JS and the GLSL loop in step (both read `TRAMPLE`).
- leaving deep water after a real swim starts the `climbOut` action in `Hiker` (played by `Character` from `Swimming To Edge`, with a vertical offset easing from the waterline up to the bank); `exitCool` stops it repeating when you bob at the edge.
- tools, felling and loose rocks are `src/props.js`. flora tiles stay placement data: anything you disturb goes through `Flora.find`/`take` (removed from the tile's items and solids, remembered by `itemKey` in `flora.removed`, which `#populate` honours when a tile is rebuilt) and then lives in props as a falling or lying tree (a one-instance copy of the species mesh from `flora.treeMesh`), a stump, or a loose rock (`rockParts` plus `rockPose`). `props.save()`/`load()` ride along in the main save; `reset()` is a new game. rock weight is by scale: under `CARRY_MAX` you can lift it, up to `PUSH_MAX` you shove it (`Props.collide`, called from the end of `Flora.collide` and only for the hiker's own position), beyond that it is bedrock. rocks stacked on another (placed off `restY`) stay put. an axe swing (`chop` in `ACTIONS`) is 0.62 s and a mature tree takes `TREE_HITS` (6) axe strokes in `props.js`: three into a wedge on your right and three into one on your left, then it falls. the first stroke takes the tree out of the forest (`flora.take`) and puts a one-instance copy in `props.cutting` whose trunk prism is replaced by a finer one (`carvedTree` in `src/cuts.js`: 36 sides, a level floor and a sloping roof per notch, cut faces wearing the aspen bark strip); every stroke rebuilds it deeper, and when it falls that same mesh falls (half-cut trees are saved in `cuts`). `trees.js` records each trunk's shape in `geometry.userData.trunk` for this. the first-person swing is sideways only when felling a standing tree: `SIDE.R` from the right for the first three strokes and `SIDE.L`, its exact mirror image, from the left for the rest (head flat, edge leading, turned between poses as quaternions); sawing a lying trunk uses the overhead chop (`VM.axe`). about four seconds of holding the button.
- the hand tool is `tools.js` (metres, grip at the origin, head +Y, edge +X). in third person `Character.#gripOf` mounts it on `mixamorigRightHand` in the straightened rest frame (see below); in first person `Props` hangs it off the camera and animates it from the action's progress. a swing plays `Stable Sword Outward Slash` (the one swing clip; `ACTION_SPAN` in `character.js` plays 0.25-1.5 s of it so the blade lands at the action's hit time, 0.4 of `dur`, mid-sweep). a proper axe clip would replace it in `ACTION_CLIP`; re-time `hits` in `ACTIONS` to match.
- retargeting straightens the arm chain first (`straighten` in `src/anims.js`): the clips are authored on a straight T-pose, and a rig whose arms rest bent or lowered (Corey's forearms bend about 30 degrees, Blaize's about 25) would carry that bend into every pose. each arm bone's rest orientation is turned to point where the clips' bone points before the world-space delta is applied. when adding a character, check the arms: pose the rig with a retargeted clip and compare bone directions with the clip's own.
- distance: tree impostor range is `flora.radius` in `quality.js` (each tile costs about 4 ms to populate and every refill walks all of them, so go easy); past it the terrain's `ALBEDO` paints a canopy from the cover map (or, for the far mesh, from how dark the satellite pixel is); valley haze is `uValley` in the shared fog in `withAtmosphere`, driven by the hour in `main.js`.
- telekinesis is the fourth tool (`T`), `src/mind.js` over `src/sculpt.js`. the ground you reshape is a separate 1 m grid of height offsets, sparse (32 m chunks in a 32x32 atlas texture behind a 464x464 index texture), clamped to +-14 m (`LIMIT`) from the lidar. `editH` (in `SCULPT_GLSL`, pulled into `HEIGHT_GLSL`) adds it in the terrain and grass vertex shaders and to the terrain normal; `World.height` adds the JS twin `Sculpt.at`. keep those two in step, and the cell size and chunk size (hard-coded as 1 and 32 in the GLSL). `CDLODTerrain` pads its min/max boxes by `LIMIT`. when an area changes, `main.js` `shaped()` calls `flora.reground(rect)` (drops the tiles so trees and rocks are replanted) and `props.settle(rect)` (loose rocks fall or ride up). sculpt chunks are saved as centimetre Int16 base64 in the main save (`sculpt`). `Hiker.channel` while sculpting swaps the walk for the Carrying clip and slows you.
- the build: `Character.#bulk` scales the chest, shoulders and arms by world-space factors (`BULK`, with `BULK_SCALE` per character) after the rig is measured, so retargeting still sees the original skeleton; head, hands and legs keep their size so the feet keep the ground.
- UI style lives in `style.css`: two brand colours, peach `#f6d3bd` (accent, primary buttons, N on the compass) and navy `#37587b` (selection fill, toggles), on neutral black and white; `--navy-light` is the tint for sky, lakes and the grip meter. every size comes from the `--t-*` scale and nothing is smaller than `--t-xs` (12 px), canvas text included (`hud.js`, `map.js`). the controls reference (`renderControls` in `main.js`) shows only the scheme in use, with a switch for the others; keep it to the essentials. the wordmark is `assets/brand/rawah.png` (the outline logo, 1400 px wide).
- `Character.#reach` lifts both arms over whatever clip plays while `Hiker.channel` is set. `CDLODTerrain` replaces the stretched satellite colour with bare earth and bedding on steep sculpted faces (`editSlope`). `Props.settle` also moves stumps and felled trees with the ground.
- posture: `Character.#upright` turns the hips so the spine runs along world up (85% of the way), while standing or walking; the retargeted clips otherwise lean about 10 degrees forward in idle. lying, sitting, climbing, swimming and the flip keep their own lean (`UPRIGHT_SKIP`).
- footprints: `pressAt` in `atmosphere.js` reads the shared trample ring (`uTrA`/`uTrB`, shared with `grass.js`); in snow it lifts the snow where something has stood and darkens what is pressed. it is shading, not geometry, so the snow surface itself does not dip. snow buries grass tufts gradually (they shrink, and only the deepest vanish).
- the boxel field is `src/boxels.js`: a 512² certified-sleeping grid of moisture (`u`, R channel) and wear (`w`, G) the ground remembers — footsteps, tire tracks, fires drying it (`dry()`), rain saturating it (`wet()`). every cell carries a proven live window so only cells that can change get integrated; `#paint` re-renders the DataTexture only when a step or event dirtied it, throttled to ~12 Hz. the terrain albedo darkens wet and trails wear (`BOXEL_COLOR` in `src/terrain.js`); grass reads the same texture in its vertex shader — wear lies blades down for good, moisture reads lusher. `sample(x, z)` gives the CPU view (valley mist feeds on damp ground at dawn). keep `uBoxel` coords as `p / uExtent + 0.5` everywhere.
- firewood is `src/wood.js`, on top of the axe felling in `props.js`: fell a tree ([T] axe, click) and it lies whole in `props.lying`; the axe on its thick end (`props.strike` hands it to `Wood.strike` when no standing tree is in reach) first takes the branches off (the lying instance is hidden and one bare trunk mesh wearing the atlas bark strip takes its place), then every two strokes saws a fire-log length off the cut end; each log flies to a pyramid stack beside the trunk. when the usable trunk is gone `props.dropLying` removes it. [U] at a stack takes an armful (pack of 16) or, at the camp woodpile, stacks the pack; [J] (or [K] away from the fire) drops the pack as a stack; [F] at the fire lays a log (+60 min), and lighting a fire costs three logs from the pack or camp pile. unfinished trunks and ground stacks are not saved (the tree comes back whole); the pack, the camp pile and the cut count are.
- coffee is `src/coffee.js`: [K] near a lit fire takes the three leaning logs off (`Fire.setTeepee`), sets a grate across it and puts a speckled-enamel percolator on it. [U] facing the pot starts a ~40 s brew, then pours six sips into the thermos tumbler; [O] sips (gamepad: ← when away from the fire, touch: Sip), adding stamina through `coffeeBoost` in `main.js` and a little warmth. the pot and thermos are saved.
- breakfast is `src/breakfast.js`: with the grate rigged, [K] again puts a cast-iron skillet of bacon and eggs on the grate's free end (child of `coffee.stand`); [U] facing it eats once cooked (perfect until `GOOD`, burnt past `BURNT`).
- the family tent is `src/famtent.js`, chosen in Settings → Tent (`settings.tent`, 'solo' or 'family') and pitched with [C] 5 m ahead (`camp.pitch(x, z, yaw, kind)`, `camp.campProblem` wants level, clear ground). `WALLS` are plan segments with doorways left open; `Hiker`, `ATV` and `Party` actors call `solids(p, r)` (`camp.collide`) and `floorAt`, wired in `main.js`. `camp.doorDist` is where scripted scenes use the door.
- a friend's calls of nature live in `Party.#business` (`companions.js`): every 5-9 minutes, when the pair is just following you, one friend picks a standing tree (`flora.treesAround`) and a spot on its far side from you, walks there, holds the `Crouch Idle 01` emote and calls `party.onDrop`; `src/dump.js` turns that into falling droppings, a small pile with flies and a buzz when you are near, shrinking away after ~200 s (not saved). dev: `__rawah.dump`, set `party.urge = 0` (after `party.endWork()`) to trigger one.
- keys added by the camp features: [U] is the one "use" key (sit, ride, take firewood, pour coffee, eat), [K] cook, [O] sip, [J] drop firewood; the gamepad's L3 is [K] (cook, or drop the pack away from the fire).
